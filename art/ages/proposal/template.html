01 / First, what we actually have

The foundation exists.

“Playable unit” and “finished age-specific artwork” are different things. This proposal makes that distinction explicit.

Stone Age · playableWorkers, Clubmen, Slingers

All three have outlined eight-direction art. Wooden Spearman is a proposed additional Stone unlock. The Stone Town Center and barracks also have new graphics.

Bronze Age · playable, mixed artSix military/support types

Swordsman and Archer upgrades use older single-direction sprites. Spearman, Mounted Spearman and Healer have outlined art. The legacy Ram still exists in the running game but is removed from this proposal. Matching Bronze models and animations are now available for art review. 04 / My recommended rules

Age unlocks. Research upgrades.

Keep advancement meaningful without instantly transforming every army into a stronger one. Buildings stay useful throughout the match.

Pay once per unit line.

  1. Finish Bronze research at the Town Center, or Iron research at the Town Hall.
  2. New unit types become trainable once their production building is complete.
  3. Research a line upgrade at its production building. For example, Clubman → Bronze Swordsman at the Barracks.
  4. All existing units in that line convert globally; new and queued recruits emerge as the upgraded version. Health percentage, orders and control groups survive. No free healing.

Earlier recruitment buttons are replaced. Wooden Spearman → Bronze Spearman follows this same paid rule. Spears remain an affordable line; cavalry flanks answer ranged armies. Military appearance changes with the paid line upgrade.

Let the settlement grow visually.

Existing buildings gain their age’s appearance automatically, keep their function and retain their current health. You do not rebuild all your farms and houses.

Functional improvements are researched separately. Masonry improves durability; Tower Reinforcement improves tower combat. No free healing, income, housing or army bonuses just for changing an age’s artwork.

Town Center → Town Hall → Castle is one continuous civic building. It renames automatically with each age. Every other building keeps its name and changes appearance; its unlock age stays as shown in the tree.

Keep research easy to find.

Settlement center: Bronze at the Town Center; Iron and Tools I at the Town Hall; Tools II at the Castle. Blacksmith: shared weapons, armor, masonry and siege research. Production buildings: upgrades for the units they train.

Recommendation: simplify the current three-step stat-tech chains into one Bronze and one Iron tier. Age progression should carry the main sense of advancement.

Keep the first version focused.

The new unit type is Ballista, upgrading to Heavy Ballista. Wooden Spearman extends the existing spear line into Stone; Crossbowman is the Iron ranged upgrade. Keep single-target healing and shots, one cavalry line, and the current three-resource economy.

Match-start military could cost food + wood, bringing gold pressure into Bronze. Spearmen remain an affordable cavalry counter. Ballistae cost wood + gold. Every cost profile and numerical advancement price in this proposal needs playtesting.

Existing research cancellation refunds 50%. Keep worker production running alongside advancement. Losing a prerequisite later does not cancel started advancement; losing its researching settlement center does.
