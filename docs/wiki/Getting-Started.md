# Getting started

Build an economy, scout your opponents, and lead an army through three ages. Start with a small map and one opponent while learning the controls.

## Your first match

1. Choose Start Game, your faction, map size and opponents in Match Setup. Easy difficulty gives you more time to react.
2. Select a Worker and right-click a tree or gold deposit. Split workers across nearby nodes, and keep paths around buildings clear.
3. Build a Farm early: food is produced automatically. Train more Workers at the Town Center, but keep enough resources for your first Barracks.
4. Build Houses before the population bar fills. Train a small mixed army, scout nearby ground, and protect your workers before expanding.
5. Select the Town Center and open Upgrades when you are ready to advance to Bronze Age.

## Winning and rebuilding

Annihilation eliminates a player only when they have no Town Center, Town Hall or Castle, no foundation for one, and no surviving Workers, including sheltered Workers. Destroying the main building alone may not finish the opponent: a surviving Worker can rebuild.

Annihilation matches that remain unresolved end in a draw after 40 minutes of game time. Economic victory requires 5000 cumulative gathered resources; spending resources does not reduce that total. Timed victory compares scores after 20 game minutes. Elimination still applies in those modes.

## Match options

Difficulty changes how often the AI makes strategic decisions and how readily it launches attacks. Easy, Normal and Hard work independently of faction and personality. Rusher, Boomer, Turtle and Balanced personalities favor different plans.

Spectate AI Battle lets you watch AI opponents with the map revealed to you; the AI still uses its own fog of war. Map size limits the number of opponents. The game supports up to eight players including AI seats.

Mutators can change the normal opening or victory conditions. Use None for the standard rules described in this manual. A map seed lets you revisit the same generated terrain.
