# Unit roster

Base values before upgrades. Distances and speed use world units; training and combat use game seconds. Each faction has seven shared lines and one exclusive unit.

## Worker

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilankt/RTS/main/assets/ui/Units/worker_outlined_icon.png" alt="Worker" width="100">

Builder and gatherer

**Progression:** Worker → Bronze Worker → Iron Worker (automatic with age)

**Trained at:** Town Center / Town Hall / Castle · **Cost:** 25 food · **Time:** 5 s

**Base stats:** 100 HP · 0–0 damage · 0 light armor · 40 movement speed · 0 attack range

## Clubman

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilankt/RTS/main/assets/ui/Units/clubman_icon.png" alt="Clubman" width="100">

Generalist front-line infantry

**Progression:** Clubman → Bronze Swordsman → Iron Swordsman

**Trained at:** Barracks · **Cost:** 70 food, 65 gold · **Time:** 8 s

**Base stats:** 250 HP · 18–22 damage · 4 heavy armor · 50 movement speed · 48 attack range

## Slinger

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilankt/RTS/main/assets/ui/Units/slinger_outlined_icon.png" alt="Slinger" width="100">

Ranged damage dealer

**Progression:** Slinger → Archer → Crossbowman

**Trained at:** Barracks · **Cost:** 55 food, 50 wood, 60 gold · **Time:** 7 s

**Base stats:** 150 HP · 12–16 damage · 1 light armor · 60 movement speed · 200 attack range

## Wooden Spearman

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilankt/RTS/main/assets/ui/Units/spearman_outlined_icon.png" alt="Wooden Spearman" width="100">

Anti-cavalry infantry

**Progression:** Wooden Spearman → Bronze Spearman → Pikeman

**Trained at:** Barracks · **Cost:** 50 food, 45 gold · **Time:** 7 s

**Base stats:** 200 HP · 14–18 damage · 2 light armor · 45 movement speed · 48 attack range

## Mounted Spearman

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilankt/RTS/main/assets/ui/Units/Ages/mounted_spearman.png" alt="Mounted Spearman" width="100">

Fast raider and archer counter

**Progression:** Bronze: Mounted Spearman → Iron: Heavy Cavalry

**Trained at:** Stable · **Cost:** 120 food, 65 gold · **Time:** 10 s

**Base stats:** 300 HP · 20–26 damage · 3 heavy armor · 90 movement speed · 48 attack range

## Ballista

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilankt/RTS/main/assets/ui/Units/Ages/ballista.png" alt="Ballista" width="100">

High-damage siege support; fragile, keep behind your army

**Progression:** Bronze: Ballista → Iron: Heavy Ballista

**Trained at:** Siege Workshop · **Cost:** 160 wood, 60 gold · **Time:** 14 s

**Base stats:** 120 HP · 85–115 damage · 0 light armor · 28 movement speed · 280 attack range

## Healer

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilankt/RTS/main/assets/ui/Units/Ages/healer.png" alt="Healer" width="100">

Automatically heals wounded nearby allies; keep behind your army

**Progression:** Bronze: Healer → Iron: Priest

**Trained at:** Temple · **Cost:** 70 food, 85 gold · **Time:** 8 s

**Base stats:** 120 HP · 0–0 damage · 0 light armor · 55 movement speed · 0 attack range

## Horse Archer

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilankt/RTS/main/assets/ui/Units/Factions/horse_archer.png" alt="Horse Archer" width="100">

Steppe ranged cavalry: fast raider; vulnerable to spears

**Progression:** Bronze Age · Steppe Clans only

**Trained at:** Stable (Steppe) · **Cost:** 130 food, 60 wood, 100 gold · **Time:** 13 s

**Base stats:** 190 HP · 14–18 damage · 1 light armor · 90 movement speed · 180 attack range

## Axeman

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilankt/RTS/main/assets/ui/Units/Factions/axeman.png" alt="Axeman" width="100">

Highland shock infantry: breaks sword infantry; vulnerable to ranged fire

**Progression:** Bronze Age · Highland Clans only

**Trained at:** Barracks (Highland) · **Cost:** 90 food, 75 gold · **Time:** 10 s

**Base stats:** 220 HP · 28–34 damage · 1 light armor · 52 movement speed · 48 attack range
