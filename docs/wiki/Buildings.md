# Buildings

Base values and earliest available appearance. Civic buildings are named Town Center, Town Hall and Castle in Stone, Bronze and Iron Age.

## Barracks

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilankt/RTS/main/assets/sprites/Buildings/Ages/1_barracks.png" alt="Barracks" width="150">

Trains infantry and ranged core units

**Requires:** Stone Age · **Cost:** 100 wood, 100 gold · **Build time:** 10 s

**Base stats:** 1000 HP · 5 fortified armor

**Trains:** Clubman / Swordsman, Slinger / Archer / Crossbowman, Spearman / Pikeman, Axeman (Highland)

## Town Center / Town Hall / Castle

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilankt/RTS/main/assets/sprites/Buildings/Ages/1_castle.png" alt="Town Center / Town Hall / Castle" width="150">

Town center and victory objective

**Requires:** Stone Age · **Cost:** 500 wood, 500 gold · **Build time:** 50 s

**Base stats:** 5000 HP · 10 fortified armor
