# Combat and worker safety

Mixed armies are stronger than a single unit type. Protect your support units, scout before attacking, and keep a route home.

## Build a supporting army

- Sword infantry forms the front line. Spearmen protect against cavalry. Ranged units need space and protection while they fire.
- Mounted Spearmen and Heavy Cavalry are mobile raiders. Use them against exposed ranged units and workers; avoid running into spears or concentrated tower fire.
- Ballistas have high ranged siege damage but only 120 base HP and no base armor. Keep infantry between them and enemy melee troops. Upgrading to Heavy Ballista improves damage and range, not durability.
- Healers automatically restore 6 HP every 1 game second to the most-wounded ally within 110 world units. Keep them just behind the fighting line and move them along with the army. Priest research adds armor.
- Neutral Healing Fountains heal nearby units from either side. Secure the area before resting a wounded army there.

## Commands, stances and formations

Right-click an enemy to focus it. Use Attack Move from the command card to advance while engaging enemies along the route. Shift queues supported orders so units can carry out a sequence.

Aggressive pursues nearby enemies. Defensive fights within a limited area. Stand Ground stays in place and attacks targets in range. No Attack is useful for retreating or scouting. Cycle stances with S and formations with F, or use the command card.

## Workers take shelter

Threatened Workers automatically try to shelter in a friendly Town Center, Town Hall, Castle or Watchtower. Shelter has limited capacity; workers without a free place try to retreat. Carried resources and the previous job are preserved.

After six safe game seconds, sheltered Workers return to their previous tasks. Manual garrison orders are also available for Workers. Use the building's Ungarrison command to release them. Military troops cannot garrison.

## Warnings and fog of war

Attacks on your forces trigger a spoken warning and flashing rings on the minimap. Click the minimap to inspect the area. L opens the event log, and Home returns to your main base.

Idle-worker alerts wait four seconds before notifying you and are rate limited. Use the idle-worker badge or F1 to find workers needing orders.

Explored terrain remains visible under fog, but current enemy activity requires vision. Combat sounds are suppressed in unexplored and fog-covered areas even if your camera is there. Visible sound effects are also limited by camera distance.
