# Ages and upgrades

Age research unlocks options and refreshes your settlement's appearance. Military unit lines require their own paid upgrades.

## Stone Age

Begin with Workers, Clubmen, Slingers and Wooden Spearmen. Your civic building is called the Town Center. Available economic buildings include Houses, Farms, Lumbermills and Mines, alongside the Barracks.

## Bronze Age

Complete three Stone Age buildings other than the Town Center. Houses, Farms, Barracks, Lumbermills and Mines count; duplicates count too. Select the Town Center, open Upgrades, and pay for Advance to Bronze Age.

Your Town Center becomes a Town Hall and existing buildings and Workers gain Bronze artwork. Stables, Temples, Watchtowers, Markets, Blacksmiths and Siege Workshops become available, subject to their building prerequisites. Cavalry, healers, Ballistas and your faction's special unit unlock.

## Iron Age

After Bronze Age, complete a Blacksmith and two different specialist buildings chosen from Stable, Temple, Watchtower, Market and Siege Workshop. Research Advance to Iron Age at your Town Hall.

Your civic building becomes a Castle. Existing buildings and Workers gain Iron artwork, and the final military-line upgrades become available.

## Train or upgrade?

Train recruits one new unit. An upgrade improves every existing and future unit in that line for your player. Advancing an age alone does not turn Clubmen into Swordsmen or Mounted Spearmen into Heavy Cavalry.

A building has separate Train and Upgrades tabs. Selecting a different building, or clearing and reselecting, returns to its default tab. Hover over a disabled option to see the missing age, building, technology, resources or population space.

Blacksmith research has six families with three levels each. Only the next available level is shown. Improvements stack with unit-line upgrades.

## Research reference

| Research | Building | Cost | Time | Effect / requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Advance to Bronze Age | Town Center / Town Hall | 400 food, 200 wood | 60 s | Requires 3 completed Stone Age buildings, excluding the Town Center. Duplicates count. Unlocks advanced buildings, units and paid unit-line upgrades. |
| Upgrade to Bronze Swordsman | Barracks | 180 food, 120 gold | 35 s | Clubman becomes Bronze Swordsman. Upgrades all your existing and future units in this line. +5 damage, +1 armor. |
| Upgrade to Archer | Barracks | 180 wood, 120 gold | 35 s | Slinger becomes Archer. Upgrades all your existing and future units in this line. +3 damage, +20 range. |
| Advance to Iron Age | Town Center / Town Hall | 800 food, 400 wood, 200 gold | 90 s | Requires a completed Blacksmith and two different Bronze specialist buildings. |
| Upgrade to Bronze Spearman | Barracks | 140 food, 100 wood | 30 s | Wooden Spearman becomes Bronze Spearman. Upgrades all your existing and future units in this line. +3 damage, +1 armor. |
| Upgrade to Iron Swordsman | Barracks | 240 food, 180 gold | 45 s | Bronze Swordsman becomes Iron Swordsman. Upgrades all your existing and future units in this line. +4 damage, +2 armor. |
| Upgrade to Crossbowman | Barracks | 240 wood, 180 gold | 45 s | Archer becomes Crossbowman. Upgrades all your existing and future units in this line. +6 damage, +15 range, -0.2 attacks/s. |
| Upgrade to Pikeman | Barracks | 200 food, 160 wood | 40 s | Bronze Spearman becomes Pikeman. Upgrades all your existing and future units in this line. +4 damage, +1 armor, +14 range. |
| Upgrade to Heavy Cavalry | Stable | 300 food, 220 gold | 50 s | Mounted Spearman becomes Heavy Cavalry. Upgrades all your existing and future units in this line. +5 damage, +2 armor. |
| Upgrade to Priest | Temple | 180 food, 180 gold | 40 s | Healer becomes Priest. Upgrades all your existing and future units in this line. +1 armor. |
| Upgrade to Heavy Ballista | Siege Workshop | 300 wood, 220 gold | 50 s | Ballista becomes Heavy Ballista. Upgrades all your existing and future units in this line. +18 damage, +30 range. |
