# Troubleshooting

The game is a playable beta. Reports with a save and clear steps help reproduce remaining problems.

## I keep running out of food

Build more Farms and slow recruitment until income catches up. Farms work automatically; assigning Workers to them does not increase output. Several active military buildings can consume food much faster than a single Town Center.

## A unit or upgrade is unavailable

Hover over the command to read its requirement. Check your age, faction, completed prerequisite buildings, earlier line upgrades, resources and population. Bronze Age unlocks special units, but only Steppe can train Horse Archers and only Highland can train Axemen.

## A Worker has stopped gathering

First check whether it is carrying cargo, returning to a drop-off, sheltering, or retrying a blocked approach. These are active tasks. If it remains idle, use F1 and right-click a reachable tree or deposit. Spread crowded workers out and leave approaches to drop-off buildings open.

## The game is slow or a match will not end

Lower the resolution, reduce game speed, or choose a smaller map and fewer opponents. Large late-game armies and tight passages remain areas of active improvement.

Look for surviving enemy Workers, a civic building foundation, or sheltered Workers after destroying the main base. They can keep an opponent alive. Unresolved Annihilation matches draw at 40 minutes of game time.

## Download and launch

For the portable version, extract the entire ZIP and run RTS/RTS.exe; do not move the executable away from its _internal folder. The installer creates a Start-menu shortcut. Neither build requires Python.

The Windows executable is not code-signed. If you are uncomfortable running a prebuilt beta, the repository includes the source and build instructions.

When reporting a bug, include the game version, map size/seed, factions, difficulty, what happened and what you expected. Add a screenshot, relevant save, or a short excerpt from debug.dat after checking it for personal information.
