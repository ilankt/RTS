# Economy and expansion

Food sustains recruitment, wood builds your settlement, and gold funds troops and research. Watch income as well as your current stockpile.

## Food comes from Farms

A completed Farm produces 5 food every 4 game seconds (1.25 food per second). No Worker needs to gather there. Most units cost food; Ballistas instead cost wood and gold.

Four Farms cover the base food cost of continuously training Workers at one Town Center. Military recruitment needs additional Farms. Build food income before opening several training queues at once. Faster game speed accelerates both production and consumption.

## Wood and gold

Right-click a tree or gold deposit with selected Workers. Workers carry resources to an appropriate drop-off and then return. Lumbermills accept wood, Mines accept gold, and your civic building can accept both.

Before upgrades, a Worker gathers up to 8 wood or 5 gold per game second while actively working. Walking, drop-off travel and congestion reduce delivered income. Place drop-offs close to resources with room to approach.

Up to 3 workers can gather from one node at full rate. Spread larger groups across neighboring trees or deposits. Workers look for nearby resources as nodes run out and retry temporary interruptions; press F1 to find anyone who still needs an order.

## Population and queues

Your base capacity is 10; each completed House adds 5. Living units and paid training orders use population space. A full queue can therefore prevent another building from recruiting even before those units appear.

A training queue lists each unit type separately. The building's progress bar shows the current recruit; later entries wait their turn. Cancelling active production refunds half its cost; cancelling a paid queued recruit refunds its cost.

## Markets and investment

At a Market, sell 100 wood or food for 50 gold, or spend 150 gold to buy 100. Trade has a cost, so use it to cover a shortfall while fixing your income.

Reserve resources for age advancement and research, but do not stop making Farms, Houses and defenses when your settlement needs them. Upgrade prices and effects are listed in the technology tables below.
