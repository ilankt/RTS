# Factions

Both factions share the core economy, buildings and seven common unit lines. Each adds one exclusive Bronze Age unit.

## Steppe Clans

Train Horse Archers at the Stable after reaching Bronze Age. Use their speed and ranged attacks to raid exposed workers, pressure Axemen and reposition. Spears, enemy cavalry and Watchtowers are dangerous counters.

## Highland Clans

Train Axemen at the Barracks after reaching Bronze Age. They deal strong damage to sword infantry but are vulnerable to Slingers, Archers and Horse Archers. Give them cover as they close the distance.

## Choosing opponents

Your faction and the AI faction are chosen separately in Match Setup. AI faction may also be Random. Faction determines the exclusive unit; personality determines the AI's priorities; difficulty changes decision cadence and attack readiness.
